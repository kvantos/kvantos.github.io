# About Me

Hi, I'm **Sergio** (aka **kvantos**).

Over a decade of wrestling with Linux systems and cloud infrastructure has taught me that most engineering problems can be solved with a sharp Python script, a bit of persistence, and a remarkably cunning plan. Throughout my career, I’ve specialized in taming wild cloud environments—whether that meant surgical interventions to slash terrifying Snowflake bills, architecting microservices that run like a well-organized court conspiracy, or building Python tools so effective at automating soul-crushing tasks that my colleagues suspect low-grade dark magic was involved. When I’m not coercing AWS servers to do my bidding or tinkering with blinking Arduino microcontrollers on weekends, I’ve guided over ten brave engineers through the dragon-infested waters of systems administration, ensuring they survived with both their code and sanity intact.

As for my daily arsenal, I move comfortably between low-level systems whispering and high-altitude data architecture. My core toolkit revolves around Python and Bash for relentless automation, paired with deep operational experience in Linux administration, AWS, and modern data platforms like Redshift and Snowflake. By combining microservices design with rigorous cost optimization and infrastructure tuning, my main goal is always the same: building systems that are robust, efficient, and cheap enough to make a medieval miser weep with joy.
